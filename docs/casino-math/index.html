Read the figures correctly

RTP is a long-run mathematical expectation, not a forecast, a guaranteed refund, a measure of volatility or a way to recover losses. Game versions and modes can have different values. Payout ratios do not determine the full blackjack house edge. No calculator can identify the next winning spin or hand.

Amounts are hypothetical currency units with two decimal places. Display rounds to cents, half up; RTP cost is the rounded complement of return. Actual settlement rules may differ. If gambling is causing financial or emotional harm, stop and seek support.
